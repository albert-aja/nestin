# Nestin — Everyday Assistant & Personal Improvement Sanctuary

> **Tagline:** Every moment, right on time.  
> **Brand Direction:** A personal **Everyday Assistant & Personal Improvement Sanctuary** where users feel at home (*"nest in"*). Combines universal alarms, smart wake-up intelligence, contextual planning, personal organization, offline personal money management, calm cognitive/curiosity growth, everyday reflection, curated wishlists, and wellness tools in a warm, privacy-respecting local-first home.  
> **Cohesive Product Guardrail:** Every feature must connect naturally to the 6 bird companions' shared personal ecosystem—no isolated standalone vertical apps (such as dedicated cooking/recipe apps, room renovation planners, or noisy arcade game halls).  
> **The Mascot Flock:** Six specialized bird companions guiding users across six core life domains: **Roo** (Time, Planning, Habits & Digital Discipline), **Scout** (Everyday Assistance & Device Utilities), **Seren** (Personal Wellness & Daily Relaxation), **Pippin** (Personal Interests & Exploration), **Twill** (Notes, Organization & Personal Money Management), and **Echo** (Journal, Memories & Reflection).

## Project Overview & Architectural Explanations

Nestin is a unified **Everyday Assistant & Personal Improvement Sanctuary** built to feel like home—integrating high-reliability alarms, intelligent wake-up gates, daily planning, digital discipline, personal knowledge organization, wellness and relaxation rituals, curiosity and hobby growth, curated wishlists, and reflective memory journaling into one cohesive space. Rather than scattering life across disconnected utility or niche apps, Nestin brings everyday structure and calm personal growth together under one roof without bloat or unwanted online dependencies.

### Core Architectural Decisions & Design Patterns

1. **Strict Feature Preservation:**
   - The entire smart alarm engine (gradual volume ramping, challenge engine with arithmetic/memory/typing gates, snooze limits, custom sound import into app sandbox, full-screen active alarm interceptor) is preserved without regression.

2. **Modular, Feature-Oriented Clean Architecture:**
   - **Framework:** Flutter 3.44+ (Dart 3.12+) targeted primarily for Android, iOS, and cross-platform mobile environments.
   - **State Management:** Reactive `ChangeNotifier` and `Provider` pattern with single-responsibility providers (`AlarmProvider`, `ReminderProvider`, `TimerProvider`, `SettingsProvider`, `SoundProvider`, `CalendarProvider`, `TaskProvider`, `RoutineProvider`, `NoteProvider`, `CollectionProvider`, `JournalProvider`, `DashboardProvider`, `WearableProvider`, `ReadingProvider`, `BucketListProvider`, `GeofenceProvider`, `BeforeYouGoProvider`, `MoneyProvider`).
   - **Persistence Layer:** Structured, local-first JSON document persistence managed by `StorageService` using `SharedPreferences` (including versioned schema migrations via `migrateMoneySchemaIfNeeded()`, atomic snapshot rollback commits via `commitMoneyLedgerAtomically()`, and full JSON backup export/restore). Guarantees zero-latency cold starts, resilient background recovery, and seamless offline functionality without SQLite migration locks.
   - **Local Backup & Restoration Engine:** Dedicated full backup serialization (`exportFullBackupJson` and `restoreFullBackupJson`) allowing users to export their entire personal life data into an offline JSON backup file and restore it on any device.

3. **Domain Abstractions & Extensibility:**
   - **Weather Abstraction:** `WeatherProviderInterface` decoupling current weather presentation from third-party APIs. Implemented with `LocalWeatherService` delivering immediate, context-aware forecasts, seasonal scenario switching (`LocalWeatherScenario`: `Auto`, `Clear & Warm`, `Rain Showers`, `Cool Breeze`, `Cold & Stormy`), live weather listener updates across Home/Explore/BeforeYouGo, and precipitation/chill threshold flags (`isRainy`, `isHeavyRain`, `isCool`, `isCold`).
   - **Sleep Cycle Engine:** `SleepCycleCalculator` computing optimal 90-minute REM sleep cycles and 14-minute sleep latency to suggest restorative bedtime and wake-up target windows.
   - **Quick Capture Integration:** Global multi-entity sheet (`QuickCaptureSheet`) allowing instant creation of Notes, Tasks, Reminders, Journal entries, or Wishlist items from anywhere in the app.
   - **Universal Real-Time Search:** Cross-module indexing (`UniversalSearchScreen`) that queries Notes, Tasks, Calendar Events, Journal entries (respecting private/custom lock masking), Collections & Packing Lists, Wishlist items, Reading List items, Bucket List dreams & Places to Visit, Location Reminders & Saved Places, BeforeYouGo Trips & Presets, Money Accounts & Transactions, App Fast Commitments, Usage Limits, Phone Modes, Automations, and Alarms simultaneously with categorized search result pills.
   - **Wearable Health Telemetry:** Decoupled `WearableHealthServiceInterface` with `LocalWearableHealthService` supporting native platform channel bridges (`com.nestin.app/wearables`) and simulated test sandboxes for step counts, sleep duration, and hydration syncing.

4. **Flock Companion Architecture & Domain Ownership:**
   - Features, screens, and empty states are organized into **6 Mascot Domains**.
   - Each mascot represents a personality archetype, visual color accent, and functional responsibility, giving users a warm, emotionally supportive everyday environment.

### Mascot Expression & Interactive System Architecture
1. **6 Standardized Expression States:** Each bird features 6 visual postures: `idle` (gentle breathing hover), `celebrate` (joyful raised wings, sparkle effects), `nudge` (helpful reminder, pointing wing), `sleepy_relaxed` (calm nightcap/closed eyes), `thinking` (analytical chin-tap for searches/NLP), and `alert_surprised` (active alarm or timer zero).
2. **24-Hour Day Cycle Handoff:**
   - *06:00 - 08:30 (Dawn):* **Roo & Scout** wake user up, enforce math challenges, and run customizable BeforeYouGo departure checklists with smart heads-up alerts.
   - *08:30 - 12:00 (Morning):* **Roo & Seren** drive Pomodoro focus sprints and pace hydration intake.
   - *12:00 - 17:00 (Midday):* **Scout & Twill** manage task progress, scheduled agenda, and structured notes.
   - *17:00 - 20:00 (Evening):* **Pippin & Twill** track hobby practice hours, reading lists, and curated wishlists.
   - *20:00 - 22:30 (Dusk):* **Echo** guides mindful reflections, daily prompt carousels, and nostalgic memories.
   - *22:30 - 06:00 (Night):* **Seren** coordinates 30-minute bedtime wind-down, procedural rain audio, and REM sleep cycles.
3. **Interactive Touch Gestures:** Single-tap triggers spring physics bounce with contextual speech bubble quips; double-tap triggers joyful 360° spin with flock chirp; long-press opens domain navigator.
4. **Contextual Dialog Engine:** Dynamic quips tailored to time of day, completion streaks, alarm solves, hydration status, and departure checks.

  - **12 Core Product Philosophy & Anti-Guilt Principles:**
    1. **Human-Centered Support:** Users feel welcomed, supported, and understood without judgment.
    2. **Non-Clinical Positioning:** Personal well-being and everyday self-support, never medical treatment or clinical diagnosis.
    3. **No AI Chatbot Companion / AI Therapist:** Zero simulated human relationships, virtual companions, or conversational AI therapy.
    4. **Optional Participation:** Users are never forced to check in, complete activities, rate moods, or report emotional progress.
    5. **No Guilt-Based Gamification:** Strictly **zero** "days clean" counters, abstinence streaks, scores, broken-streak warnings, punitive resets, or failure labels.
    6. **Personal Autonomy:** Users choose what they want to work on and which activities feel meaningful, with the freedom to pause, skip, or exit any screen at any time.
    7. **Privacy First:** Local-first on-device storage inside `StorageService`; emotional writings, behavioral goals, and setback reflections stay private on-device with zero automated psychological diagnosis of free text, zero ad targeting, and zero covert URL/app surveillance.
    8. **Practical Support:** Focus on concrete, realistic actions users can apply immediately in everyday life rather than abstract theory.
    9. **Clear Human Boundaries:** Recognize when self-help is insufficient and make reaching out to trusted people (and verified professionals/crisis resources in Level 4) natural and stigma-free.
    10. **Inclusivity & Non-Religious Neutrality:** Respect diverse lifestyles, cultures, beliefs, and personal values without making assumptions or introducing dedicated religious menus; personal practices are supported indirectly via Nestin's general-purpose tools.
    11. **Simplicity:** Contextual, cohesive flows rather than an overwhelming collection of disconnected wellness widgets.
    12. **Independence:** Help users build their own coping strategies and autonomy rather than fostering dependency on Seren.

- **Entry Points, Privacy Lock Boundary & Three-Layer Calm Menu Architecture:**
  - **Zero-Friction Reachability:**
    - Exposed as the **primary hero hub** inside Seren's wellness domain, surfaced on `ExploreHubScreen` and `HomeDashboardScreen` via `SerenWellnessSummaryCard`.
    - Reachable in 1 tap from anywhere in Nestin via a **"Need a Moment?" / "Mental Aid"** shortcut inside `QuickCaptureSheet` and deep link `nestin://mental_aid`.
  - **Privacy & Lock Boundary:**
    - Top-level **Mental Wellness Support** menu and acute **Emotional First Aid** / **Urge Support** tools remain immediately accessible without a lock gate so distress relief is never blocked.
    - Sensitive personal records (Break the Cycle commitments, trigger/setback reflections, personal support plans, and saved Echo diary reflections) respect Echo's existing `DiaryLockHelper` PIN/biometric protection.
  - **Three-Layer Calm Menu Structure:**
    1. **Layer 1 — Immediate Support (*"What would help right now?"*):** Fast, calm triage for acute distress (`EFA`) and active urges (`BTC-02`).
    2. **Layer 2 — My Comfort Kit & Trusted Support:** 1-tap user-curated calming tools (`EFA-07`), comfort memories/notes/bookmarks, and Trusted Contacts (`GS-01`–`GS-03`) with editable starter & custom message templates.
    3. **Layer 3 — Everyday Resilience, Habit Change & Wellness Tools:** Break the Cycle plans (`BTC`), Self-Understanding educational guides & Echo reflection prompts (`SU`), Healthy Alternatives (`HL`), and direct access to Seren's existing Relax & Unwind (`SerenRelaxationScreen`) and Health/Sleep/Hydration tools.

  The six proposed areas operate as one cohesive, cross-linked experience rather than six isolated mini-apps (e.g., Emotional First Aid offers breathing from Relax & Unwind; Break the Cycle suggests activities from Healthy Living; Self-Understanding opens Echo's journal; Get Support links to a trusted person), evaluating carefully which capabilities warrant dedicated screens versus contextual entry points:

  - **Area A — Emotional First Aid (`EFA`: Immediate Emotional Support):**
    - **`EFA-01` — How Are You Feeling? (Non-Clinical Felt-State Triage):** Welcomes users without requiring explanations or mood ratings via 6 primary felt-state doors (*I'm feeling overwhelmed*, *I'm feeling anxious*, *I'm having a difficult day*, *I can't stop thinking*, *I feel lonely*, and *I just need a little comfort*), expanded optional states (*Discouraged*, *Angry*, *Exhausted*, *Racing thoughts*, *Sad*, *Frustrated*, *Restless*, and *Not sure how I feel / "I just need a moment"*), plus a direct tool bypass link (*Sounds • Breathing • Break the Cycle • Trusted Contacts*).
    - **`EFA-02` — Guided Support Flow (`Acknowledge → Pause → Ground/Calm → Reflect → Choose a Small Next Step`):** Non-linear, skippable guided flow tailored to the chosen state. For example, selecting *Overwhelmed* offers a grounded acknowledgement (*"You don't have to figure everything out right now. Let's take things one step at a time."*), 4 practical response buckets (*Calm my body*, *Quiet my surroundings*, *Get it out of my head*, *Take one small step*), and 6 concrete choices (*Take a slow breath*, *Try a grounding exercise*, *Listen to something calming*, *Write down what's on your mind*, *Think about one small thing you can do*, *Reach out to someone you trust*).
    - **`EFA-03` — Grounding Exercises:** Interactive, eyes-friendly sensory grounding activities including **5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Grounding**, identifying surrounding objects, feeling physical contact with the floor/chair, noticing environmental sounds, and gentle awareness exercises.
    - **`EFA-04` — Breathing Exercises:** Non-medical, optional paced breathing sessions (slow breathing, box breathing, 4-7-8 breathing, gentle paced breathing, short breathing breaks) reusing Seren's breathing engine with instant stop/exit controls if uncomfortable.
    - **`EFA-05` — Emotional Reset:** Collection of 2–5 minute reset activities (short calming soundscape, guided pause, gentle stretching prompt, grounding exercise, quiet reflection, or healthy distraction).
    - **`EFA-06` — Small Next Step:** Helps overwhelmed users pick one manageable micro-action rather than solving everything at once (drink water, step outside, take a short walk, tidy one small corner, send someone a message, take a break, write down one concern, or return to the task when ready).
    - **`EFA-07` — Comfort Kit:** User-controlled personal shelf pinning favorite Seren soundscapes, relaxation/grounding exercises, saved readings, personal encouragement notes, Echo gratitude pebbles/memories, local comfort photos, and preferred external resource bookmarks for 1-tap access.
    - **`EFA-08` — Gentle Encouragement:** Short, grounded, realistic messages acknowledging difficult experiences without toxic positivity, overly motivational slogans, minimizing statements, generic platitudes, or false promises (*"everything will be fine"*).
    - **Emotional First Aid UX Guardrails:** Never force explanations or mood ratings; never assume an activity helped just because the user completed it; never automatically interpret emotional writing; never lock users into a rigid sequence; allow skipping every optional step; and avoid unsolicited follow-up notifications after difficult experiences.
    - **Emotional First Aid — 4-Level Maturity Roadmap:**
      - *Level 1 — Immediate Comfort (MVP / 100% Local):* Felt-state selection (`EFA-01`), empathetic non-toxic response (`EFA-02`, `EFA-08`), grounding (`EFA-03`) & breathing (`EFA-04`) exercises, relaxing soundscapes, private/ephemeral writing space, and starter Comfort Kit (`EFA-07`).
      - *Level 2 — Personal Support Space (v1.1 / 100% Local):* Saving favorite exercises/stories, writing personal encouragement notes to future self, pinning comforting local photos or Echo memories (`MemoryItem`) and external resource bookmarks into the Comfort Kit, Trusted Contacts with prewritten & custom message templates, user-pinned preferred support shortcuts, and Morning/Evening rituals (integrating Roo's `RoutineProvider` + Seren's `SerenRelaxationProvider`).
      - *Level 3 — Everyday Emotional Resilience (v1.2 / Offline-Bundled):* Curated finite educational library (`SU-08`) on stress, emotions, personal boundaries, self-compassion, and habit mechanics; guided reflection exercises; optional personal reflection history via Echo's `TimelineScreen`; and pull-based gentle routine suggestions (zero nags or streaks).
      - *Level 4 — A Bridge to Human Support (Future Backend-Supported Scope):* Expanding beyond local Trusted Contacts into guidance on when professional help is appropriate (`GS-06`), verified counselor/support-service directories (`GS-04`), and locale-aware regional crisis & emergency resources (`GS-05`) maintained via Nestin's NestJS/Supabase editorial pipeline.

  - **Area B — Break the Cycle (`BTC`: Non-Judgmental Behavioral & Addiction Self-Management):**
    - **Behavioral Nuance & Problem Framing:** Helps users regain control over unwanted repetitive behaviors that interfere with daily life—such as excessive pornography consumption, doomscrolling, compulsive gaming, uncontrolled spending, repetitive social media checking, or excessive video consumption—without shaming or automatically labeling them as "addictions." Recognizes diverse contributors (boredom, negative emotional states, stress, loneliness, emotional discomfort, environmental triggers, learned behavioral patterns, avoidance, easy access to stimulating activities, difficulty managing urges, and situational/social influences), avoids treating psychological and physical mechanisms as completely separate categories, and centers on whether a behavior causes persistent loss of control, distress, or meaningful everyday interference.
    - **`BTC-01` — Personal Change Goals (*"My Commitment + My Why"*):** Users define behaviors they want to **reduce, stop, manage, replace, or better understand** alongside their personal motivation (*"Why this matters to me"*), setting their own expectations with zero imposed abstinence goals or addiction diagnoses.
    - **`BTC-02` — Urge Support & Mindful App Pause (Intention Check):**
      - *Immediate Urge Support:* Low-friction 2–5 minute intervention (*"You don't have to decide what to do for the rest of your life right now. Let's focus on the next few minutes."*) offering Urge Surfing, Environment Reset, a short distraction break, calming audio, trigger reflection, reaching out to a trusted person, or choosing an alternative activity—with zero lengthy lectures during acute urges.
      - *Mindful App Pause for Ambiguous Apps:* Distinct from Roo's strict `AppFast` / `FocusLock` timers, users can select ambiguous apps (such as web browsers or social apps) to trigger a calm 3–5 second intention check via `DeviceControlPlugin` foreground window detection, displaying their own written *"Why"* (with configurable cooldown grace windows and vulnerable-hours schedules). Because Nestin never inspects URLs or browsing content, opening an app is never logged as a failure—users choose between **"I have a clear purpose — Continue"** (zero penalty) or **"I'm feeling an urge — Help me step back"** (launching Urge Support).
    - **`BTC-03` — Urge Surfing:** Guided 5-step observation flow (*1. Notice the urge → 2. Identify what it feels like → 3. Observe without judging yourself → 4. Focus on breathing or grounding → 5. Decide what to do next*) without promising that an urge will disappear after a fixed duration.
    - **`BTC-04` — Trigger Reflection:** Optional logging of circumstances surrounding an urge or behavior (time, manually entered location, current activity, optional emotional state, environment, possible trigger, and what happened immediately before) with zero automated diagnosis or cause inference.
    - **`BTC-05` — Habit Pattern Journal:** Private reflection prompts (*What happened?*, *What was I doing before it happened?*, *How was I feeling?*, *What did I need at that moment?*, *What might I try differently next time?*) stored directly inside Echo's private diary.
    - **`BTC-06` — Healthy Alternatives:** Contextual activity suggestions grouped into 7 user-need categories connecting naturally across Nestin birds:
      - *Stimulation:* Creative hobbies, puzzles, learning something, exploring new activities (Pippin/Scout).
      - *Relaxation:* Ambient sounds, breathing, guided relaxation, quiet time (Seren).
      - *Movement:* Walking, stretching, light physical activity (Seren/Scout).
      - *Connection:* Contacting friends, spending time with family, participating in community activities (Get Support / Scout).
      - *Mental Escape:* Reading, listening to stories, enjoying hobbies, watching something intentionally (Pippin/Seren).
      - *Accomplishment:* Completing a small task, organizing something, learning a skill (Roo/Twill).
      - *Creative Expression:* Drawing, writing, music, photography (Pippin/Echo).
    - **`BTC-07` — Environment Reset:** Practical physical-space shift prompts (leave the current room, put the phone away / in another room, change activities, move to a shared space, reduce exposure to a known trigger, take a short walk). Optional website/app-blocking capabilities remain explicitly separated from the default experience and require explicit user consent, clear privacy boundaries, and platform feasibility.
    - **`BTC-08` — Personal Boundaries:** User-defined, adjustable everyday boundaries (e.g., *No social media during meals*, *No phone in bed*, *Set a spending limit* linked with Twill, *Establish gaming hours*, *Avoid specific triggers in certain situations*).
    - **`BTC-09` — Recovery Reflection:** Shame-free post-setback reflection when a user voluntarily logs a slip—explicitly avoiding *"You failed"*, *"You lost your streak"*, *"Start again from zero"*, punitive indicators, or shame-based notifications, affirming that a setback never erases previous effort.
    - **`BTC-10` — Change Progress:** Non-competitive qualitative progress indicators (personal reflections completed, strategies tried, goals adjusted, situations better understood, alternative activities explored, personal milestones) with zero mandatory streaks, abstinence scores, or public comparisons.
    - **`BTC-11` — Relapse Prevention Plan:** Private user-authored preparation plan listing known triggers, difficult situations, go-to alternative actions, personal boundaries, trusted contacts, helpful resources, and a compassionate post-setback plan.
    - **Break the Cycle Product Boundaries:** Positioned strictly as self-management and behavioral-change support—must never claim to cure addiction, diagnose behavioral disorders, guarantee recovery, assume every unwanted behavior is an addiction, shame users for setbacks, automatically monitor browsing/app activity, or replace qualified professional care.

  - **Area C — Relax & Unwind (`RU`: Audio-First Calm & Sleep Sanctuary):**
    - Reuses Seren's existing relaxation architecture (`SerenRelaxationScreen`, `SerenRelaxationProvider`, `AmbientSoundService`, `SerenTtsService`, `SerenVisualCanvas`) directly as embedded steps inside Emotional First Aid, Urge Support, and the Comfort Kit while preserving audio-first preferences (passive visuals optional, zero games, zero social feeds, zero AI companion, zero mandatory morning/evening locks, full audio-interruption/headphone/fade-out/resource-cleanup support):
      - **`RU-01` Ambient Sound Library** & **`RU-02` Sound Mixer** (25-sound procedural library + downloadable seasonal packs, multi-track layering up to 6 tracks, per-track/master volume, mute/unmute, favorites, recently played, search/categories, offline access).
      - **`RU-03` Sound Distance** (`Near` / `Balanced` / `Far` acoustic EQ rolloff & spatial reverb) & **`RU-04` Stereo Position** (`Left` / `Center` / `Right` constant-power panning).
      - **`RU-05` Saved Soundscapes** (9 curated presets + custom user mix save/rename/edit/duplicate/delete CRUD).
      - **`RU-06` Guided Relaxation** & **`RU-07` Breathing Sessions** (coherent 5-5, Box 4-4-4-4, 4-7-8, gentle morning 4-2-4, progressive muscle relaxation, body scan, mindfulness, sleep preparation—optional and stoppable anytime).

      - **`RU-12` Mindful Activities** (optional gratitude/reflection prompts routed to Echo), **`RU-13` Playback Timer** (15/30/45/60/90m + custom sleep timer with 30s smooth fade-out), **`RU-14` Morning Relaxation** (first-hour-after-waking Morning Peace customization), & **`RU-15` Evening Wind-down** (sleep prep accessible at any time, never locked to clock hours).

  - **Area D — Self-Understanding (`SU`: Reflection & Awareness via Echo):**
    - **Integration Rule (Echo Owns Journaling):** Seren never creates a duplicate diary database; instead, Seren provides contextual prompts and specialized reflection entry points across Emotional First Aid, Break the Cycle, Relax & Unwind, and Self-Understanding that persist cleanly inside Echo's `JournalProvider` (`DiaryLockHelper`):
      - **`SU-01` — Emotional Journal:** Private writing entry point into Echo's locked diary.
      - **`SU-02` — Voice Journaling:** Spoken reflection capture routed to Echo's audio memo capability where feasible.
      - **`SU-03` — Guided Reflection:** Optional structured reflection prompts (*What is on your mind?*, *What made today difficult?*, *What made you feel better?*, *What would you like to approach differently?*, *What are you grateful for?*).
      - **`SU-04` — Let It Out:** Unstructured free-writing space requiring no templates or questions, offering a dual completion choice: **"Save privately to Echo's Diary (Locked)"** or **"Let it go (clear & don't save)"** (ephemeral release leaving zero trace on disk).
      - **`SU-05` — Unsent Letters:** Private space to write messages the user does not intend to send without communication pressure, offering the same dual **Save to Echo (Locked)** vs **Let It Go (Don't Save)** choice.
      - **`SU-06` — Daily Reflection** & **`SU-07` — Optional Mood Check-in:** Completely optional reflection and mood logging (never a mandatory daily mission, streak, or prerequisite to unlock Seren features).
      - **`SU-08` — Educational Library:** Curated, offline-bundled, plain-language, factually reviewed guides covering *Understanding emotions*, *Stress*, *Self-compassion*, *Personal boundaries*, *Behavioral patterns*, *Habits*, *Emotional regulation*, *Coping strategies*, and *Everyday well-being*—always stating clear non-clinical limitations.
      - **`SU-09` — Personal Insights:** User-controlled reflection collections, user-defined observations, and optional personal record summaries—explicitly excluding automated psychological diagnoses, personality labels, or symptom correlation analytics.

  - **Area E — Healthy Living (`HL`: Cross-Bird Everyday Well-Being Launcher):**
    - Connects emotional well-being with practical everyday habits across Nestin without building a standalone fitness or nutrition vertical:
      - **`HL-01` — Healthy Alternatives Library:** Categorized everyday alternatives (*Take a walk*, *Read something*, *Stretch*, *Listen to music*, *Spend time outdoors*, *Contact a friend*, *Try a hobby*, *Take a screen break*) shared across Emotional First Aid and Break the Cycle.
      - **`HL-02` — Small Daily Actions:** Low-pressure single actions (*Open the curtains*, *Drink water*, *Get fresh air*, *Tidy a small space*, *Stretch*, *Take a short break*, *Spend time away from screens*) with zero mandatory daily checklists.
      - **`HL-03` — Personal Routines:** Links to Roo's `RoutineProvider` and shared reminder system for optional morning, evening, relaxation, screen-free, or personal reset routines.
      - **`HL-04` — Sleep Habits**, **`HL-05` — Hydration**, & **`HL-06` — Physical Activity:** Connects directly to Seren's existing `SleepWellnessProvider`, `HydrationProvider`, and `HealthWellnessProvider` (non-medical bedtime wind-down, simple water reminders, everyday walking/stretching/sitting breaks—explicitly excluding nutrition/medical prescription advice and Strava-style GPS running/sports analytics).
      - **`HL-07` — Personal Well-being Goals:** Flexible, user-controlled lifestyle intentions (e.g., *Spend more time outdoors*, *Reduce evening screen time*, *Establish a relaxing evening routine*, *Take more breaks*, *Explore new hobbies*) without punitive streaks or pressure.

  - **Area F — Get Support (`GS`: Human Connection & Future Safety Bridge):**
    - **Initial Local Scope (`GS-01`–`GS-03`, `GS-06`, `GS-07`):**
      - **`GS-01` — Trusted Contacts:** Local-only list of trusted friends, family members, or mentors.
      - **`GS-02` — Quick Contact:** 1-tap access to call or message a trusted contact via native device dialer/SMS/WhatsApp intents.
      - **`GS-03` — Message Templates:** Editable low-pressure starter templates (*"I'm having a difficult day. Can we talk?"*, *"I don't need advice right now, but I could use someone to listen."*, *"Could you spend some time with me?"*, *"I'm struggling and would appreciate some help."*) plus a **"+ Write My Own Template"** editor so users can save phrases in their own natural voice (EN/ID/casual style).
      - **`GS-06` — Professional Help Guidance:** Non-stigmatizing educational guide helping users understand when professional support may be appropriate and what to expect (never framing professional care as failure).
      - **`GS-07` — Optional Support Plan:** Private local plan storing trusted contacts, preferred ways to ask for help, personal coping strategies, helpful resources, and post-setback / emergency steps.
    - **Deferred Backend Scope (`GS-04`, `GS-05` — Level 4):**
      - **`GS-04` — Support Directory** (verified counselors, psychologists, and mental health/support organizations) & **`GS-05` — Crisis Resources** (accurate, location-aware emergency & crisis lifelines) are deferred to Level 4 when dedicated verification and maintenance workflows are active.
    - **Crisis Safety Requirements (Safety Overrides Ordinary Flows):**
      - Never present Seren as a substitute for professional treatment or assume breathing/relaxation is sufficient during an acute crisis.
      - If a user indicates immediate danger, suicidal intent, or serious safety risk, prioritize immediate human support and appropriate location-aware emergency/crisis resources—never ordinary motivational messages or generic relaxation prompts.
      - Never claim a simple questionnaire or automated tool can reliably detect or certify someone's safety; any future automated free-text risk interpretation must be treated as a separate, safety-critical decision with dedicated evaluation, uncertainty handling, and safeguards (strictly outside initial scope).

- **Religion, Spirituality & Lifestyle Inclusivity Architecture (Cross-Flock Product Decision):**
  - **Why Dedicated Religious Menus Are Excluded:** Following careful evaluation across all six Nestin characters, Nestin deliberately avoids building dedicated religion or spirituality modules due to 7 operational and product-integrity factors: (1) inter-religious diversity across traditions and practices, (2) internal diversity across denominations and regional interpretations, (3) authoritative theological review required for content accuracy, (4) scripture/translation/audio copyright licensing, (5) ongoing calendar/observance maintenance, (6) product identity drift away from a general-purpose everyday companion, and (7) multi-system architectural complexity.
  - **Supporting Personal Beliefs via General-Purpose Nestin Capabilities:**
    | User Activity | Existing General-Purpose Nestin Capability |
    | :--- | :--- |
    | Waking up for morning prayers or personal practices | **Roo** — alarms and routines (`AlarmProvider`, `RoutineProvider`) |
    | Remembering observances or meaningful personal dates | **Shared Calendar** (`CalendarProvider`) |
    | Recording prayers, intentions, or spiritual reflections | **Echo** (`JournalProvider`) and **Twill** (`NoteProvider`) |
    | Creating reminders for personal practices | **Shared Reminders** (`ReminderProvider`) |
    | Establishing calm morning or evening practices | **Roo & Seren** — routines (`RoutineProvider`, `SerenRelaxationProvider`) |
    | Saving preferred external reading or audio links | **Shared External Content Bookmarks** (`Comfort Kit`, `ReadingProvider`) |
    | Participating in meaningful community activities | **Pippin & Scout** — hobbies, activities, and local exploration |
  - **What Nestin Explicitly Will Not Build:** Scripture databases, dedicated religious reading systems, prayer-time calculation engines, religious audio libraries, religion-specific educational catalogs, internally maintained religious observance calendars, belief-based user profiling/classification, features assuming a user's faith, or authoritative religious guidance.
  - **Cross-Flock Guiding Principle:** *"Build capabilities for universal human needs rather than separate systems for every belief system."* Religious and nonreligious users share identical general-purpose capabilities without segmentation.
