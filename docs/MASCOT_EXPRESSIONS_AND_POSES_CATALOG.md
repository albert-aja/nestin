# Nestin — Whole-Project Mascot Expressions & Poses Catalog

> **Purpose:** Comprehensive inventory of all **108 existing mascot sprites** plus **130+ proposed new mascot expressions, poses, props, and duo interactions** across every module in the Nestin ecosystem (**Roo**, **Scout**, **Seren**, **Pippin**, **Twill**, and **Echo**).
>
> **Art & Localization Rule:** All new mascot sprites MUST be rendered as **pure pose/expression illustrations with zero baked-in text** (`RGBA` transparent PNGs). Any speech quip, badge label, or sound effect is rendered dynamically by Flutter using localized keys (`l10n.tr('...')`) in English (`en`) and Bahasa Indonesia (`id`).
>
> **Non-Banking Rule (Twill):** Nestin is strictly a personal everyday and manual money management app, NOT a banking or payment app. All Twill money poses use tactile personal props (coins, paper ledger, abacus, envelopes, ceramic piggy bank, balance scale, shopping basket) and never depict credit terminals, QRIS scanners, wire transfers, or banking institutions.
